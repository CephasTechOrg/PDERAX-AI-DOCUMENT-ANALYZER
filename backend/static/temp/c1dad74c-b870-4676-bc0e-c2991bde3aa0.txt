SUMMARY:
--------------------
This 2024 research by Jeffery Jones computationally validates Newtonian gravitation and Keplerian orbital models using modern astronomical datasets. The study implements numerical integration techniques (Runge-Kutta 4th Order and Velocity Verlet) in Python to simulate planetary and satellite orbits, comparing results against high-precision data from NASA JPL Horizons and AGI's Satellite Tool Kit.

The research demonstrates exceptional accuracy, with RMS positional errors below 0.01% and energy conservation within 0.02% over one-year simulations. Velocity Verlet integration showed superior performance with only 6.8 km positional error and 0.02% energy drift. These results confirm that classical two-body dynamics remain highly accurate for low-eccentricity, low-perturbation systems like Earth's orbit around the Sun.

The study establishes a crucial benchmark for testing advanced orbital models and defines the boundary conditions where Newtonian mechanics remains sufficient before perturbative or relativistic effects become significant. This work provides a foundation for validating new computational methods and serves as a null model for quantifying higher-order effects in orbital dynamics.
